# Isolated UX prototype

Before work, have the human confirm the objective, user/scenario, fixed constraints,
two or three distinct interaction hypotheses, comparison criteria, side effects,
time/run/cost bounds and stop conditions. Route through `investigate-codebase`
for baseline discovery, then use the host's supported isolated workspace execution
for the explicitly authorized experiment. A read-only investigator cannot become
a writer merely because this playbook was selected. If the host cannot supply an
isolated writable worktree plus separate ports, database and seed, stop and report
the missing capability; never write prototypes into the product tree.

Use the same scenario and seeds for every option. Show error, empty and loading
states where relevant. Two color variations alone are not distinct UX hypotheses.
Keep actual preview, diff, logs, outcomes, counterevidence and unknowns. Compare
task achievement separately from subjective preference. A rejected hypothesis is
an exploration result, not a completed product task. Stop at budget, sufficient
discrimination or diminishing evidence.

Prepare `decision-packet/v1` with at most three alternatives and the current
input/task hashes. Revalidate all pins and the selected packet digest on resume.
Preserve previous packets, including rejected/held reasons in the existing human
decision owner. Human concept decisions use the signed concept-test source;
unverified recommendations remain recommendations. Only a chosen, normally
reviewed and approved task may return through ship for product implementation.
