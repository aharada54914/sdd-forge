## The Critical — my cycle-6 fix did not close the bypass

I asked this evaluator to assume the fix was incomplete and try to break it.
It did, and it was right.

The fix narrowed the heading capture to `[ \t]*`, which covers exactly two
of the characters the boundary tolerated. Measured end-to-end on a scratch
clone: a padded `## Output Paths And Hashes` heading using form feed,
vertical tab, NBSP, NUL, or a space/FF pair still returned
`IMPLEMENTATION_REPORT_OK` from the report validator AND `REVIEW_CONTEXT_OK`
from the boundary, with `plugins/SMUGGLED-SECRET.md` in the authorized set.
The table heading was still open for one byte too — NUL. Worse, the two
headings are matched by *different* rules at the boundary, and cycle 6
tightened only one of them, so `## Output Paths And Hashes ` with a plain
trailing space was still honoured while `## Outputs ` was not. The claim in
the cycle-6 report that "the two cannot disagree again" was false.

The suite did not catch it because its loop enumerated exactly `space tab`
on one heading, and the mutation that would have fixed it — `[ \t]*` → `\s*`
— survived the whole suite.

   report validator is demonstrably **not** a precondition of authorization,
   which is exactly what makes the Critical reachable rather than
   defence-in-depth.

## Remediation Landed on This Branch (post-verdict, same day)

- **The Critical is closed, and this time verified against the evaluator's
  own attack set.** The report validator now rejects control characters in
  a heading outright (NUL included, which `str.strip()` does not remove) and
  strips the remaining whitespace over the full Unicode set; the boundary's
  LEGACY matcher is now exact like its sibling. **Twenty-case matrix — both
  headings × ten pad forms (none, space, tab, CR, FF, VT, NBSP, NUL,
  space+FF, FF+space): zero bypasses remain.**
- The suite's pad loop is extended from two forms to seven, with per-class
  expectations (control characters get their own diagnostic), so the
  whitespace class is pinned rather than two samples of it. Mutants killed:
  control-character guard removed, strip removed, and strip narrowed back to
  space+tab.
- The boundary's two matchers are now pinned in
  `template-validator-parity.tests.sh` (19/0), with both prefix-form
  regressions mutation-killed — that pin did not exist before, which is why
  cycle 6's half-fix went unnoticed.
- Major 2's first half is fixed by adding the two missing top-level lines;
  the evaluator's second observation — that report validation is not a
  precondition of authorization — is a real architectural gap and is
  recorded for a WFI rather than patched here.
- Major 1 stands as recorded: the boundary fix genuinely is outside T-006's
  scope. It belongs to a security WFI of its own, not to this task, and
  attributing it here would repeat the scope creep the evaluator charged.
