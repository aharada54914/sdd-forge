check at `:2011`).** The AC-026 "optional keys absent" guard uses
`(Get-PropSafe $concept $key) | Should Be $null`, which cannot distinguish an
absent key from a present-but-empty array. REQ-005(f) / AC-026 require the three
keys to be absent ("3 キーをいずれも持たず"), not merely empty. Measured:
injecting `"must_not_own": []` left the Describe at 4 passed / 0 failed, while
`"must_not_own": ["delivery quantity"]` correctly failed at `:1976`.

Not blocking: the fixture genuinely omits all three keys today (lines 1729-1749
read directly), so AC-026 is proven at this commit; the gap is future-drift
detection only. The populated side at `:1769-1771` uses
`@(...).Count | Should BeGreaterThan 0` and does not share the weakness.
Classified `Deferred`.

**Minor 2 -- `tests/sdd-domain/contract-v2-schema.Tests.ps1:2057.**
`@($fixtureTruncatedJson, (New-OversizedContractFixture ...), $fixtureV1Contract).Count | Should Be 3`
is a tautology as a count assertion: under `Set-StrictMode -Version Latest`,
`@($a,$b,$c).Count` is 3 for any three scalars and still 3 when one is `$null`;
only an undefined name fails. It verifies that three names resolve, not that
T-002 contributes exactly three fixtures.

Concrete consequence: if T-002 later adds a fourth boundary fixture and
`Get-T005NegativeFixtureBodies` (`:2018-2037`) is not updated, the total stays 73
(`:2066` green), the corpus stays 78 (`:2073` green), and the AC-013 parity loop
silently omits the new fixture -- exactly the "a fixture was dropped or
duplicated" divergence the `### Done When` "Fixture total" item exists to catch.

T-003's 62 and T-004's 8 are derived from the live `$structuralFixtures` /
`$crossReferenceFixtures` arrays and do not share this weakness. So the
implementation report's claim at `T-005.md:102-107` that "the total cannot
silently diverge" holds for **70 of the 73**, not all 73. Classified `Accepted`;
recorded as a follow-up rather than fixed in-gate, because changing it after a
PASS-grade cycle would require a further cycle to certify and the exposure is a
future-drift blind spot, not a present defect.
