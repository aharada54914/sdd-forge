## Finding — earlier completed rounds disappear from caller validation when both failed-round keys are used (Major)

The candidate permits a contract containing both `historical_failed_rounds` and `failed_final_round` (`fresh-review-recovery.py.candidate:149-155`). For an earlier failed multi-round attempt, the helper appends its completed rounds to `completed_rounds` (`:234-245`). If the immediately preceding attempt also failed in a later round, the later-round branch **assigns** `completed_rounds = last['completed_rounds']` (`:319-334`), discarding the earlier entries. Both normal callers validate only the completed rounds returned by the helper (`plugins/sdd-review-loop/scripts/spec-review-precheck.sh:404-408`, `.ps1:617-622`). Thus, a repinned malformed earlier completed contract can escape the full `validate_contract` check in this permitted combination. The local failed-attempt inspector checks its verdict/identity fields but explicitly delegates full completed-contract validation to its caller (`failed-last-attempt.py.candidate:1-5,146-164`); evidence pinning and ledger checks do not replace that validation.

The isolated fixture only constructs an earlier failed round-3 attempt followed by an immediately preceding round-1 no-output partial (`attempt17.fixture.py:33-51`), and its completed-round assertion checks that one shape (`:142-149`). Its malformed-contract caller case also runs only that shape (`:102-123,172-177`). The reported 18 PASS therefore does not cover the allowed combined-key branch.

Minimum correction: extend rather than replace `completed_rounds` in the later-round branch, preserving ordered entries from every partial attempt. Add a combined-key fixture with completed rounds in both failed attempts; repin a structurally malformed contract in the earlier attempt and assert both Bash and PowerShell prechecks reject it through their full contract validator. Keep all earlier evidence pins and historical verdicts unchanged.
