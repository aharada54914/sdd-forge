## Summary

Rewrote step 5 of `design-sync-loop/SKILL.md` from its DS-29-era explicit
no-op ("This feature defines the point and performs no check at it...") to
an active three-branch check point that names `design-sync-scan.sh` (or
`.ps1`) and `specs/<feature>/mockups/` as its target, and states, for each
exit code:

- **Exit 0** (clean): continue directly to step 6, no additional prompt, no
  delay beyond the scan's own run time; records `Egress-Scan: clean` and
  `Egress-Scan-At` (ISO-8601).
- **Exit 1** (finding(s)): presents the findings report to the human before
  any push; an explicit override records `Egress-Scan: overridden` and
  `Egress-Scan-At`, authorizes nothing beyond *that* scan's disclosed
  findings (not a standing exemption), and a fresh scan after any
  regeneration requires its own override decision even when it reproduces
  identical findings; absent an explicit override (silence, non-response, or
  an agent's own judgment never counts), no push occurs, nothing is written
  to `Design-Source` as an override, and the agent remediates before
  re-entering the step -- explicitly distinguished from `Egress-Consent`'s
  own decline/withdrawal vocabulary.
- **Exit 2** (tool error): unconditionally blocking, with no override
  affordance offered at all, worded as a tool failure so it cannot be
  mistaken for a finding.
