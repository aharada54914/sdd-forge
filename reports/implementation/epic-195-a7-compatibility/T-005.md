shared driver extension (`_loop_trace_emit`/`assert_capability_applicability`/
`assert_event_trace` and their PowerShell twins) fully satisfy T-005's own

`tests/loops/loop-inventory.json` carries `capability_applicability` on
the `quality-gate` entry only, in the exact documented three-key shape;
the registry stays at 8 entries; a pre-epic-195 copy with the field
stripped still validates against the unbumped `loop-inventory/v1` schema
(`TEST-008.1`). `assert_terminal`/`assert_artifacts_schema`
(`Test-LoopTerminal`/`Test-ArtifactsSchema`) are confirmed byte-identical
to their pre-task form by function-body SHA-256, with a negative
self-check proving that comparison is not vacuous.
`_loop_trace_emit`/`Write-LoopTraceEvent` is the sole appender to
`_LOOP_EVENT_TRACE`, assigning one trace-wide monotonic `seq`; `loop_fixture_init`/
`Initialize-LoopFixture` resets both the trace and the sequence counter
per fixture drive. `assert_capability_applicability`/
`Test-CapabilityApplicability` reads
`.loops[] | select(.id == $loop_id) | .capability_applicability[$fixture_state]`
and is the only function in either runtime that reads the new field
(mechanically confirmed by `grep`), and is itself the `quality-gate-outcome:
capability-applicability` producer call site design.md's own per-kind
producer table names. `assert_event_trace`/`Test-EventTrace` is a pure
comparator — it never calls the appender and never mutates trace state —
that normalizes both the observed and golden traces through the identical
per-kind value-normalization rule before comparing `kind`, `producer`,
`value`, and count, rejecting each dimension's mismatch independently.

`assert_event_trace` deliberately does not wire a producer call site
inside `assert_terminal`, `drive_review_round`,
`_loop_reserve_review_context`, `check-quality-gate-cycle-limit.sh`,
`select-agent-model.sh`, or `loop_validator_skip` — design.md's own
per-kind producer table describes the full eventual wiring, but T-005's
own Done When requires the two named legacy functions to stay
byte-identical, and T-005's own Out of Scope excludes `TEST-018`/
`TEST-019` (T-006/T-007), the cases that will actually drive rounds
through those call sites.
