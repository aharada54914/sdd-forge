## Summary

Implemented the deterministic REQ-003/REQ-006 live-host-proof validator and
completed the security-review corrections. The engine strictly validates the
five-cell record schema, RFC 8785 signing targets, Ed25519 signatures and
subgroup membership, issuer-signed nonce issuance, replay/TTL/cell binding,
raw-capture and expected-config digests, classification authenticity, the
case-specific three-state SKIP gate, and atomic/idempotent nonce consumption.
It reports only `discharged`, `pending`, or a named hard error.

The sh and PowerShell entry points now use the repository's shared
byte-preserving Python dispatchers. The deterministic suite is registered in
both run-all drivers and in the apply-ready staged workflow after T-003. The
live protected workflow remains byte-unchanged.
