## Summary

Implemented T-003 and left it ready for the independent quality gate. Both
resolver runtimes now construct the complete `ownership_input`, invoke the
landed Epic A1 canonicalizer, and emit its prefixed SHA-256 under ADR-0021's
`context_binding.ownership_digest`. They also emit `resolver.version` and a
canonical `resolver.rule_set_revision` derived from the matcher-semantics
version.

The digest is independent of the changed-path/Feature projection and includes
every component include/exclude rule, every shared-path rule, and the matcher
semantics version. The semantic comparison used by the acceptance suite
explicitly removes `ownership_input`, `context_binding`, and `resolver`, so a
digest-only refresh does not by itself stale a Feature.

Added separate Bash and PowerShell ownership-digest suites rather than editing
`tests/component-path-resolver.tests.{sh,ps1}`. That existing suite is
hash-declared by Done tasks T-001 and T-005; keeping T-003 assertions separate
avoids invalidating their declarations and matches T-003's Planned Files.

The live and installed guard predicates were re-evaluated during quality-gate
cycle 1 remediation. They protect the workflow, but neither protects
`tests/run-all.sh` nor `tests/run-all.ps1`. The ownership suites are therefore
registered directly in both live run-all drivers, while only the workflow
candidate remains under `specs/epic-191-a3-path-ownership/human-copy/`. The
withdrawn run-all candidates and their manifest rows are gone, and the
remaining human-copy manifest verifies in full.

Quality-gate cycle 2's two TEST-049 Majors are remediated with the repository's
accepted shallow-aware two-form guard. Full-history checkouts strictly inspect
the pinned T-003 commits for version-surface touches; genuine depth-1 checkouts
enforce the synchronized plugin-version content invariant preserved by
