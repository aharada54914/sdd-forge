## Problem Evidence

Three features are simultaneously unable to reach their quality gate, and
in all three the immediate cause is the act of closing a review finding.

| Feature | `check-workflow-state` diagnostic | Amendment that caused it | What that amendment closed |
|---|---|---|---|
| epic-194-a6-lite-integration | `spec top-level contract hashes are stale` | `9997091c` widened `acceptance-tests.md` AC-010 from a four-target to a five-target payload set | Two Major findings raised independently by both vendor slots against T-001 |
| epic-195-a7-compatibility | `spec top-level contract hashes are stale` | `7652d01b` reworded `acceptance-tests.md` AC-009 | A cross-model panelist finding on T-005's producer identity |
| epic-196-a8-integration | `task reviewer manifest input hash is stale` | `43b237fa` narrowed `design.md`'s activation predicate to AC-006 | The single Critical that put impl-review attempt 3 into BLOCKED |

Each amendment was the correct response to its finding, and in epic-194's
case there was no finding-closing route that avoided it: `tasks.md` item 3
and `acceptance-tests.md` AC-010 disagreed on the payload set, and both are
frozen, so one of them had to move.

The consequence is not local. `quality-gate`'s Step 4 invokes
`check-workflow-state` **without** `--feature`, over every registered
specification, so a feature that is red at this gate blocks its own gate
run entirely — the task under review is never reached.

epic-196 shows the pattern at its sharpest. `43b237fa` closed the Critical
that had deadlocked impl-review. With `--opening` (commit `0732ec97`) the
impl stage now passes, and the very next gate in walk order fails on the
task stage's pinned `design_sha256` — invalidated by the same commit that
made the impl stage passable. The fix and the new blocker are one commit.

epic-193-a5-capability-resolver is **not** an instance of this class,
though it presents identically. Its `task plan hash is stale` comes from a
re-bind that pinned the raw hash of `tasks.md` rather than the
stage-normalized one; the normalized form is invariant under `Status:`
transitions and the raw form is not, so three ordinary lifecycle flips
broke it while the file's body never changed. It is recorded here only to
mark the boundary: a stale-pin diagnostic is not by itself evidence of
this defect.

## Root Cause Hypothesis

A stage contract pins the documents its review read, so that a later
reader can prove the approval was given against those bytes. That is the
right property. What is missing is any lane that owns **re-establishing**
it.

The workflow can amend a frozen document — review findings routinely
require it, and this repository has done it deliberately and with
disclosure at least three times this month. But every route that amends
one leaves an earlier stage's pin dangling, and there is no operation
anywhere in the toolchain that re-issues a stage contract against the
amended bytes. `grep -rlniE 're-?freeze|re-?pin'` over `plugins/` and
`docs/` returns exactly one file, a review ticket, and no script.

So the defect class is: **closing a review finding by amending a frozen
document invalidates the pin of an already-passed earlier stage, and no
lane is responsible for restoring it.**

WFI-027 describes the adjacent class — an approved artifact whose truth
value depends on another epic's merge state, with no lane re-checking it.
That WFI is about a contradiction existing. This one is about what happens
when you resolve one: the resolution is itself a state change no lane
owns. epic-196 is an instance of both, which is why it surfaced first
there.

The two obvious shortcuts are both wrong and should be named so they are
not proposed later:

- **Mask the change.** `RT-20260712-003` resolved a superficially similar
  stall by teaching the task-stage normalization to delete column-0
  `Second Approval:` lines. That was correct because the line is absent at
  freeze time and its later presence carries no content. Here the
  documents' bodies genuinely changed. Normalizing that away would make
  the pin assert something false.
- **Repin in place.** Rewriting the contract's recorded hash to the new
  bytes asserts that the review read text it never saw. That is forgery of
  provenance, and the pin exists precisely to make it impossible.

## Proposed Change

| Target File | Change Description |
|---|---|
| `plugins/sdd-quality-loop/scripts/check-workflow-state.{sh,ps1}` | Distinguish "the pinned document changed" from "the pinned document is missing or unreadable" in the diagnostic, and name the specific document and both hashes. Today `spec top-level contract hashes are stale` does not say which of the two pinned files moved, so every diagnosis starts by re-deriving it by hand. |
| `plugins/sdd-review-loop/skills/*-review-loop/SKILL.md` | Add a step to the finding-remediation path: when a remedy amends a document an earlier stage pinned, the loop must record which stage's pin it invalidates, in the round's own artifacts, at the time of the amendment. The information is free at that moment and expensive later. |
| New: a re-approval lane | Define the one operation that legitimately restores a pin: re-running the affected stage's review against the amended documents, producing a new contract at a new attempt. Whether that is a narrowed re-review or a full one is the design question this WFI exists to settle; what must not exist is a route that produces a new pin without a review behind it. |
| `AGENTS.md` | State the rule that makes the above enforceable: an agent may amend a frozen document to close a finding, and may never re-issue the pin that amendment invalidates. The second half is a human decision because it is a re-approval. |

## Expected Effect

The cost of closing a finding stops being paid twice — once to make the
change and once, much later and by a different session, to discover the
feature can no longer reach its gate. The stall becomes visible at the
moment it is created rather than at the next gate attempt.

## Verification Metric

Target-Metric: metrics.features_blocked_at_workflow_state_by_own_remediation
Expected-Direction: decrease
Horizon: within the next 6 features that amend a frozen document to close a review finding
Baseline: 3 of 3 such amendments in August 2026 (epic-194 `9997091c`, epic-195 `7652d01b`, epic-196 `43b237fa`) left their feature unable to reach `quality-gate`, and none recorded that it had done so
Target: 0 amendments that leave an invalidated pin unrecorded; the count of features actually blocked may stay above zero, since re-approval is a human decision and may legitimately be pending

Note the metric deliberately measures **recording**, not blocking. An
amendment that correctly invalidates a pin and says so is the workflow
behaving properly. Counting blocked features would create pressure to
avoid amending frozen documents, which is the opposite of what is wanted.

## Verification Plan

The next feature that amends a frozen document to close a review finding
is the test case. Confirm both halves:

1. **The invalidation is recorded at the time it happens**: that round's
   own artifacts name which stage's pin the amendment invalidated, and
   the commit that makes the amendment says so in its message.
2. **Detection is real, not ceremonial**: in a scratch copy, amend a
   pinned document without recording anything, and confirm the lane
   refuses or flags it. A lane that records the invalidation only when
   the author remembers to has added a convention, not a mechanism, and
   this WFI must then be classified Rejected.

The re-approval lane, if it is built, needs its own third check: a new
stage contract must be impossible to produce without a review having run.
Constructing one by hand and confirming the gate rejects it is the test.

## Rollback-Plan

The diagnostic change and the SKILL.md step are revertible by reverting
the commit whose message contains `WFI-039`; neither alters what the gate
accepts, only what it says and what the loop records. The re-approval lane
is a new capability rather than a modification, so rolling it back means
not adopting it — the three features above stay blocked pending human
re-approval, which is the current state.
