## Root Cause Hypothesis

WFI-005 aligned the template with the two consumers its evidence inventory
named (evaluator launch boundary, evidence-bundle validator) and pinned that
alignment with a parity suite scoped to those two — but
`validate-implementation-report.sh`, the same-plugin authoring-time
validator created in `9ad373e5` in agreement with the then-bullet template,
was absent from that consumer inventory, and the "additive" wording in the
Proposed Change concealed that the executed change was a replacement. The
only drivers that exercise the validator live in test suites not registered
in CI (`turn-first-workflow` runs only via `run-all.sh`, itself
unregistered), so the break produced no signal, while dual-heading-era
reports papered over the contradiction at authoring time until the
template-only form became universal.

### Regressed — 2026-08-19 retention check (`epic-192-a4-facet-manifest`)

The registered recurrence condition matched. Its second clause reads: "…or the
report required a format-retrofit commit to be accepted by the validator."
Commit `8fd365c7`, `gate(epic-192-a4-facet-manifest): fix T-003 Outputs
binding, reserve seq0760`, is exactly that — a retrofit commit against an
implementation report that had already been committed, made solely so its
`## Outputs` table would bind.

The mechanism differs from the original defect and is worth stating precisely,
because the difference is where the next fix has to go. In 2026-07-23 the
validator rejected conforming reports outright and said so. Here it said
nothing: footnote markers appended to two Outputs rows stopped those rows from
matching the strict `| \`path\` | \`hash\` |` form, and the non-matching rows
were skipped in silence. Five remediation files consequently never entered the
seq0759 manifest, and the omission surfaced only when the evaluator could not
find artifacts the report claimed to cover — one cycle later.

So the class this WFI was meant to close is intact in the loud direction and
open in the quiet one. A validator that rejects a whole report is recoverable;
one that drops individual rows without a diagnostic converts a formatting slip
into a missing-evidence finding at the next gate. Reported as friction pattern
FP-01's sibling in
`reports/retrospective/2026-08-19T043118Z-epic-192-a4-facet-manifest.md`; the
follow-up belongs in a new WFI covering silent row-level skips, not in this
one, whose Proposed Change and Rollback-Plan both address the rejection path.

Row removed from `retention-checklist.md` per the skill's retention procedure.

### Five-whys

1. **Why was a retrofit needed again?** Reports being gated lacked the identity
   and declaration elements the evaluator boundary and report validator now
   require.
2. **Why did they lack them?** Each report conformed to the contract as it
   stood when written. The contract -- template, validator, boundary -- was
   strengthened afterwards.
3. **Why did strengthening the contract strand existing reports unnoticed?**
   Contract changes were applied prospectively to the validator and template;
   nothing measured their effect on the committed corpus.
4. **Why did nothing measure it?** The validator runs on demand against the one
   report a gate needs. No standing test sweeps the corpus. Measured at
   regression time: **124 of 203 committed reports hard-fail the current
   validator** -- the disease at full scale, discovered one report at a time,
   months late, always at gate time.
5. **Root cause:** the contract and the corpus are two surfaces that must
   agree, and nothing asserts that they do. Only the contract side is updated;
   the corpus silently strands until a gate happens to step on a stranded
   report. This is the same one-surface-updated shape as WFI-036 (rule vs
   mechanism), WFI-037 (guidance vs allowlist) and WFI-038 (report contract vs
   boundary) -- the fourth instance of the pattern in this repository.

### Countermeasure (root-cause, not symptom)

`tests/implementation-report-corpus.tests.sh` -- a contract/corpus ratchet.
The 124 currently-stranded reports are frozen in
`tests/fixtures/implementation-report-corpus-baseline.txt`; the baseline may
only shrink. A report outside the baseline that hard-fails goes red at the
moment it is stranded (or committed non-conforming), not months later at gate
time; a baseline entry that starts passing, or whose file is gone, goes red
until its line is deleted. Both directions proven non-vacuous by mutation.
The prior fix widened the validator to accept the legacy form; it treated one
stranded cohort, not the stranding mechanism. This treats the mechanism.

The retention-checklist entry for this WFI is removed per the retrospective
procedure; the recurrence condition is superseded by the standing ratchet,
which is strictly stronger than a checklist row read once per retrospective.
