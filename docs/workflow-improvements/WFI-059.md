## Problem Evidence

The verification contract's `checks[].evidence` field has no single canonical
path grammar, and the two consumers resolve it differently:

- `check-contract` / `check-evidence-bundle` (the deterministic Done chain)
  resolved evidence paths written **spec-relative**
  (`verification/qg/shared/regression-20260828.log`) — the epic-194 contracts
  carried exactly that form and `check-task-state` reported all four tasks
  passing on 2026-08-28.
- `prepare-panelist-input.{sh,ps1}` resolves the same field **project-root-
  relative**, marks the spec-relative form unresolvable, and embeds the
  literal annotation `[contract names this evidence path but no file exists
  there]` into the sanitized panel bundle.

Field consequence, measured on the 2026-08-28 round-1 re-panel (bundles
`c1e97647`/`aac5658a`/`753117c9`): **all six blind verdicts** (two vendors ×
three tasks) returned NEEDS_WORK with the same dominant Major — "required
checks are recorded passes:true but the declared evidence is absent from the
bundle" — against evidence that existed, passed the Done chain, and was
merely written in the grammar the other consumer accepts. One full panel
round (six verdicts, two vendors, three bundle regenerations) was consumed
by a path-form disagreement.

The unblocking fix (`8dbce679`) rewrote the pointers into full
`specs/<feature>/…` form and relocated shared evidence into per-task
directories; round 2 then resolved every path (0 `no file exists there`
markers).

### Cost

A complete wasted panel round; three misleading Major findings recorded
against a healthy task; and a contract grammar that still permits authoring
the ambiguous form today — nothing rejects it at write time, so the trap
re-arms every time a contract is reissued by hand.

## Why-Why Analysis

| # | Why (question) | Because (answer) | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Why did all six round-1 verdicts carry the same evidence-absent Major? | The bundle told them the evidence did not exist: the preparer embedded `no file exists there` for the regression and placeholder-scan pointers. | round-1 verdict JSONs; bundle annotations |
| 2 | Why did the preparer say that about files that exist? | It resolves `checks[].evidence` project-root-relative, and the pointers were written spec-relative. | `_ppi_extract_contract_evidence_paths` resolution vs contract bytes |
| 3 | Why were the pointers written spec-relative? | The Done chain's own validators accepted that form — the author had a green `check-task-state` as confirmation the grammar was right. | 2026-08-28 `Task state check passed for 4 task(s)` on the spec-relative form |
| 4 | Why do the two consumers disagree? | The evidence-path grammar was never specified; each consumer picked the resolution base natural to its own working directory. | no grammar note in the contract template or either script |
| 5 | Why did the disagreement surface only at the panel? | The Done-chain consumers and the preparer are exercised at different workflow stages, and no test feeds one contract through both. | first joint exercise was this feature's re-panel |

## Root Cause Hypothesis

`checks[].evidence` has two resolvers and no specification. Whichever form an
author writes, one consumer accepts it and silently validates the workflow,
and the other consumer later presents the same form as missing evidence to a
blind reviewer who cannot know the difference.

## Proposed Change

1. Specify the grammar once: evidence paths are project-root-relative
   (`specs/<feature>/…`), matching the majority of shipped contracts.
2. `check-contract` gains a deterministic rejection for evidence paths that
   do not resolve from the project root (fail-closed at authoring time,
   where the fix is one line, instead of at panel time, where it costs a
   round).
3. `prepare-panelist-input` keeps its current resolution but upgrades the
   embedded annotation to name the attempted base
   (`no file exists at <resolved-absolute-path>`), so a future mismatch is
   diagnosable from the bundle alone.

## Expected Effect

A contract that passes the Done chain is guaranteed resolvable by the panel
preparer; the evidence-absent Major class from path form alone becomes
unreachable.

## Verification Metric

- A fixture contract with a spec-relative evidence path: before — passes
  `check-contract`, produces a `no file exists there` bundle annotation;
  after — rejected by `check-contract` with a message naming the required
  base.
- A fixture contract with the canonical form: unchanged PASS through both
  consumers, and the panel bundle carries the evidence content.

## Verification Plan

Twin regression test in `tests/` feeding one fixture contract through
`check-contract` and `prepare-panelist-input` in both runtimes, asserting
the matrix above; registered in run-all.

## Rollback-Plan

Revert the `check-contract` rejection (one guard clause per twin) and the
annotation wording; no stored artifact formats change. Existing shipped
contracts already use the canonical form after `8dbce679`.
